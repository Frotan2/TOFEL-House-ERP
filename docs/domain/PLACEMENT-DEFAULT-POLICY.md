## 1. What can be determined professionally

**Adopt as design requirements without another business vote:** six separately evidenced skills; governed/versioned content and answer keys; blueprint-constrained randomization; preserved attempts; deterministic objective marking and human speaking/writing rubrics; no negative marking; no mandatory overall composite; independent release; explicit uncertainty; minimal private evidence; accessible alternatives; no fake Student/Enrollment; and no mixing with academic assessment, official/mock TOEFL scores, CEFR or external certification. Selection/security algorithms and native extension points are engineering responsibilities.

**Evidence basis:** ILTA's current guidance calls for purpose-relevant assessment, trained raters, transparent scoring/conditions, accommodations, fair appeals, protected data and evidence before claiming form/mode equivalence. See §§4.2–4.6 of the [ILTA Guidelines for Practice](https://www.iltaonline.com/page/ILTAGuidelinesforPractice2024). These principles support the controls here; **ILTA does not prescribe the item counts, minutes, cutoffs, ages or retention periods below**. Those are practical local starting proposals, not official TOEFL/CEFR standards or measured psychometric properties.

**Approval boundary:** academic cutoffs and curriculum mapping, service population/delivery commitments, retake entitlements, review staffing and personal-data retention affect learners or institutional obligations. They require approval in §7. Legal requirements cannot be approved away. Business approval alone does not establish validity, legal compliance or readiness to deploy.

## 2. M01 — academic instrument and placement standard

### Recommended initial blueprint

Use a fixed, broad-screening form with the following **90-minute candidate-work budget**. Book **120 minutes** including 10-minute familiarization, a 10-minute break after listening, and 10 minutes of administration/transition allowance. Assessor marking is outside candidate time. Splitting speaking into a supervised appointment is permitted within 48 hours; total candidate work still cannot exceed its approved budget.

| Skill | Allocated evidence | Candidate limit | Marks / method |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Vocabulary/language knowledge | 12 independent scored response units: contextual choice, bounded gaps and matching | 8 min | 12 objective points |
| Grammar | 12 units: choice, gaps, sentence completion/transformation with approved answers | 8 min | 12 objective points |
| Reading | 3 passage task-sets × 6 questions | 18 min | 18 objective points |
| Listening | 3 audio task-sets × 6 questions | 16 min | 18 objective points |
| Writing | Short functional message, then a connected explanation/opinion; allow simple-to-complex responses | 30 min | Two human-rated tasks; no automatic word-count grade |
| Speaking | Brief interaction, description/narrative and follow-up reasoning | 10 min | Human-rated interaction and extended response |

The 60 objective points are **not** an overall placement score. A matching set contains multiple scored units; it is not counted as one item merely to satisfy quotas. Three locally authored difficulty strata—entry, core and stretch—each contribute 4 units in vocabulary/grammar and 6 in reading/listening. Include distinct topics across the three task-sets. These are provisional editorial difficulty labels, not calibrated ability bands. Bank feasibility checks must respect passage/audio groups and exact point/time budgets.

Default navigation: revisit answers within the active section, not after its submission. Play each listening stimulus twice on a controlled schedule; playback is included in its section time, with no candidate-controlled extra replay. Familiarization is unscored and uses separate content. No dictionaries, translation, grammar correction or generative assistance unless an approved accommodation preserves the intended construct. No surprise format or software requirement on test day.

**Why:** the structure samples all required skills while remaining practical for a center appointment; several stimuli and productive tasks reduce dependence on one topic. It cannot establish precise subskill ability from 4–6 items per stratum. Trial actual reading/audio length, candidate completion and fatigue before adopting the time limits; shorten/redesign tasks rather than silently extend one candidate's ordinary test.

### Rubrics and candidate-level recommendation

Use a **local 0–4 criterion scale**: 0 = no demonstrated response on a valid opportunity; 1 = fragmentary/highly dependent; 2 = partly effective with recurrent breakdown; 3 = generally effective and sufficiently controlled; 4 = consistently effective with appropriate range. Missing/invalid evidence is a separate status, never 0. Publish task-specific descriptors and benchmark performances before use.

- **Speaking:** communicative task fulfilment, interaction/coherence, grammatical/lexical control, intelligibility. Do not reward imitation of a particular accent.
- **Writing:** task fulfilment, organization/cohesion, grammatical control, lexical appropriateness. Do not penalize handwriting aesthetics or typing speed as language proficiency.
- Weight the four criteria equally for a **within-skill summary**, retaining each criterion. Average the two writing tasks equally; speaking summarizes evidence across its tasks. Never average speaking and writing into an unqualified overall pass. Surface large task/criterion disagreement for review.

**Proposed internal intake profiles:** Foundation (supported everyday communication), Developing (routine connected communication), Consolidating (independent connected explanation), Extended (sustained, more complex communication). These are proposed local labels, **not existing courses, official scales or CEFR equivalents**; adopt actual center terminology if it already exists. Do not manufacture four Programs to match this table.

**Proposed screening threshold:** at each relevant difficulty stratum, at least **70%** of valid objective points in each prerequisite-critical skill, plus speaking and writing averaging **at least 3/4**, with no prerequisite-critical criterion below **2/4**, provides evidence to *review* readiness for the associated next curriculum stage. Provisional route: below entry → Foundation review; entry met → Developing review; entry and core met → Consolidating review; all three met → Extended review. A high productive rating on an easy prompt cannot demonstrate a higher stage: the task and rubric anchors must address that stage's demands. Non-monotonic or uneven profiles require review, not forced routing.

These are **review triggers, never an automatic placement ladder**. One objective response from a threshold, any criterion within one rating point of a required minimum, mode concerns or a mismatch with educational history triggers second review/targeted evidence. With these short strata, uncertainty will be common. Display actual counts alongside percentages; do not invent confidence intervals or statistical equivalence.

**Required mapping artifact:** Academic Owner signs a table of actual native Program/Course IDs, internal intake label, prerequisites, which skills/criteria matter, acceptable evidence and support/reassessment routes. Recommend the highest course whose actual prerequisites are demonstrated, with reasons; no course match means “academic review required,” not fabricated enrollment. Do not automatically push uneven learners into the lowest course or conceal weak speaking/writing behind grammar strength.

**Validation gate:** before consequential use, a curriculum panel must review cutoffs against real entrance tasks and anchor performances; supervised trials must examine timing, rater consistency, item behavior and digital/paper outcomes across the intended proficiency/age range. Track later course-fit feedback separately from placement scores. Academic approval is required for the resulting mapping/cutoffs. Sample sufficiency depends on the intended claim; no arbitrary sample size guarantees validation. Until adequate evidence exists, outcomes remain reviewed provisional recommendations, not precision-certified levels. Any live trial needs separate data/operational authorization.

## 3. M02 — delivery, identity and accommodations

**Default:** supervised center-based digital objective/writing work; live assessor speaking; matched-blueprint paper/interview/hybrid option without a policy penalty; validate mode comparability rather than assuming equivalence. Retain recorded digital speaking as a supported option when M05 permits its use. Defer unsupervised remote testing and offline synchronization; neither is essential to demonstrate multi-mode capability.

Verify identity at registration and each component session. Accept sighted official ID where available; **do not require or retain an ID scan by default**. Where absent, use documented registration/contact verification plus an Identity Owner-approved corroboration route; for minors use verified guardian/authorized-delegate evidence as legally appropriate. Shared phone/email is contact, not proof of identity. Unresolved identity holds release, not an automatic accusation or a fake identity. Legitimate native candidate-account/email arrangements must be confirmed before digital account-dependent delivery; supervised physical delivery must remain a genuine alternative, not staff-password sharing.

Offer familiarization, accessible text/input controls, quiet setting and assistive access as standard. **Proposed accommodation starting point:** up to 25% extra candidate-work time where justified by functional need, adjusted by section; approved rest breaks stop the affected clock. This is not an entitlement ceiling: the Academic Owner may approve more time or equivalent tasks when necessary. Collect minimum functional evidence, not an automatic medical dossier. A transcript does not automatically preserve listening; a scribe/keyboard substitution does not automatically preserve writing. Approve the construct-preserving alternative in advance; changed-construct evidence must be labeled and reviewed, not silently compared.

## 4. M03 — validity, retakes and interruptions

**Proposed service policy:** recommendations valid for **90 calendar days from assessment completion**, provided no material change in skills/circumstances is known. Latest valid, reviewed, released decision governs; an unfinished newer attempt does not replace it. Expiry requires reassessment, not retroactive alteration of history.

- Ordinary full retake: after **14 calendar days**, normally no more than **two valid completed attempts in a rolling 90 days**. Academic Owner may authorize an earlier/additional attempt with documented learning, accessibility or course-fit reasons. Incident-invalidated attempts do not consume this allowance. No best-score/section cherry-picking.
- Targeted reassessment: only for a documented borderline/invalid component, within **7 days** of the original assessment, with unaffected evidence no more than **14 days old**, unchanged compatible policy and no integrity concern. Otherwise use a full retake. Preserve both component versions and record why retained evidence is still valid.
- Exclude the candidate's delivered item/stimulus families for **90 days**; never silently relax exclusions for bank shortage. Engineering must size pools/exposure caps against planned volume; hold/rebook if safe equivalent forms are unavailable.
- Verified interruption of **up to 5 minutes**, with supervision and response custody maintained: resume the same form with only verified lost time restored. Longer interruption or uncertain custody: hold the affected component for an equivalent replacement within 7 days; void the entire attempt only if the integrity of all components is compromised. Suspected misconduct requires review, not automatic failure. The earlier attempt remains recorded.
- A genuine elapsed time limit seals accepted work. A blank on a valid delivered objective question follows the published zero rule; undelivered/corrupt evidence does not. Never treat server outage as candidate inability or silently backdate a late upload.

These limits balance practice effects, practical scheduling and fairness; they are proposed local entitlements, not evidence of a universal 14-day learning interval or 90-day stable proficiency. Fees/refunds are outside this gate; do not infer that reassessment charges are approved.

## 5. M04 — moderation, delegation and release

**Default staffing proposal:** blind double-rating of all speaking/writing during initial calibration; reduce to **20% randomly sampled routine cases** only after the Academic Owner records adequate rater consistency. Always second-review borderline/uneven outcomes, appeals, conflicts, integrity flags and any criterion disagreement of **2 or more points**. Do not reduce sampling simply because a set number of candidates has passed through. Unrecorded interviews needing independent evidence use a second live observer or a short independent follow-up—not a purported re-rating of unavailable audio.

Assessors may score assigned work but cannot independently approve their own final release. A qualified reviewer signs the decision; unresolved disagreement goes to Academic Owner adjudication, not silent averaging. A change of role does not remove personal conflict. Absence of an eligible reviewer holds release.

**Proposed service targets:** ordinary release within **two center working days after all evidence is complete**; notify learners of holds and the expected review date. Appeals accepted within **seven calendar days of release**, with an independent response normally within **five working days**. Late appeals based on newly discovered error or access barriers remain reviewable. Publish skill findings, recommendation, rationale, validity and appeal route—not reusable keys or unrestricted evidence downloads.

Accountable roles remain: **Academic / Academic Affairs Manager** (instrument/review), **Admissions & Student Records Manager** (identity), **Administration / Records & Privacy Officer** (data/rights). They may designate qualified deputies with explicit limits. No individual names are needed now; live actions later require auditable individual assignments.

## 6. M05 — population, recording, privacy and rights

**Proposed initial population:** adults and older adolescents **aged 16+**, with age-appropriate, non-sensitive topics. This is a proposed rollout boundary, **not legal adulthood**. Younger learners require a separately reviewed age-appropriate blueprint before use; the architecture still supports them. If the center routinely serves under-16 learners, include that blueprint in the initial scope rather than applying this instrument unchanged. Confirm actual population and applicable jurisdiction—neither is inferred from location or the center's name.

No routine video, biometric identity matching, room surveillance recording or recordings for marketing/model training. Default speaking is live with structured notes. Where recorded digital speaking is chosen, retain **audio only**, for assessment/moderation/appeal purposes, with approved lawful basis and clear notice; provide an equivalent live route when recording is declined or inappropriate. Do not assume consent is always sufficient or freely given. Guardian contact/payer status alone grants neither authority nor full records access. Verify lawful authority, restrictions and expiry; give accessible learner information and assent opportunities where appropriate. Adult disclosures require lawful authority, not automatic family access.

**Proposed retention schedule, subject to jurisdiction and records review:**

| Record | Proposed ordinary limit | Rationale/control |
|---|---|---|
| Speaking audio | 90 days after result release | Covers ordinary appeals; no indefinite voice archive. |
| Non-audio raw responses, paper scripts/scans and detailed assessment evidence | 180 days after release | Supports re-marking and incident review while limiting sensitive storage. |
| Released result, criterion-score summary, rationale, versions and decision/access audit | 3 years after last activity on the placement case | Supports disputes and longitudinal course-fit explanation without keeping all raw media. |
| Abandoned/voided attempt evidence | 90 days after closure | Avoid indefinite retention where no result is released. |

A pending appeal, investigation or legal hold suspends relevant deletion; record its basis and review it periodically. Privacy owner resolves conflicts with statutory duties before adoption. Content definitions/keys needed to interpret retained decisions remain versioned and restricted; after lawful raw-evidence deletion retain a deletion event and acknowledge that full re-marking is no longer possible. Backups need a bounded documented retention schedule, restricted restoration and reapplication of deletion/holds; deleting the live file alone is insufficient. Never claim immediate erasure from exported copies or backups.

Permit access by assessment purpose, current assignment and lawful recipient; correction/deletion/export requests go to the Records/Privacy Owner. Use center-authored or expressly licensed questions/media only; record permission for digital delivery, printing, adaptation and retention, plus expiry. Public availability, a textbook purchase or an official-exam label is not evidence of reuse rights. Do not copy official TOEFL items or import an unlicensed bank. An organizational legal/records review must validate notices, age/guardian rules and retention before affected operation.
