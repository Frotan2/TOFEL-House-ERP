## 1. Professional determinations — no further business vote

Keep the managed/versioned bank, six skill profiles, constrained randomized forms, objective marking and human speaking/writing assessment, protected evidence, explicit invalidity, historical attempts and independent release. No mandatory overall composite, negative marking, fake Student/Enrollment, academic-result write, official/mock TOEFL score, CEFR mapping or certification.

Use curriculum-relevant tasks, trained assessors, transparent conditions and uncertainty review. Form, mode and rater comparability must be investigated, not inferred from equal question counts. These principles follow the [ILTA Guidelines for Practice, §§4.2–4.6](https://www.iltaonline.com/page/ILTAGuidelinesforPractice2024); that guidance does **not** validate any local cutoff, duration, age boundary or retention period.

**Revise the earlier defaults as follows:**

## 2. Final approval set

All five packages are approved. Organizational owners must still supply the delegated artifacts. Delegation is not approval of missing rules, factual assumptions or legal compliance.

### F01 — Curriculum authority and course-placement policy (M01)

**Recommend:** criterion-referenced, human-reviewed placement into existing courses; no universal percentage cutoff or invented level ladder. Recommend the highest suitable course whose actual prerequisites are evidenced. Uneven or uncertain evidence leads to a documented support/reassessment recommendation, not automatic admission or a forced lowest-level assignment.

**Your decision:** confirm use of the existing course catalog and authorize **Academic / Academic Affairs Manager** to sign the course-entry mapping and assessment specifications, or identify a different institutional placement policy. Supply/delegate access to the actual catalog and entry expectations; do not approve invented course IDs.

**Required owner artifact before the affected implementation gate:** course/level → prerequisite skills → tasks/rubric benchmarks → decision/review rule, with reasons and version. Where numeric rules are justified, they must be course-specific and supported by standard-setting and trial evidence before consequential use. Trial results must address timing, age suitability, rater consistency and form/mode behavior. An unresolved map cannot be replaced by 70%. Any live trial requires separate data/operational authorization.

### F02 — Learner population and delivery commitment (M02/M05)

**Recommend:** supervised center-based digital, paper/interview and hybrid delivery, with no penalty for the supported mode; defer unsupervised remote delivery. Plan a **120-minute appointment containing up to 90 minutes of active assessment**, with accessible familiarization and breaks. Accommodations may exceed the ordinary booking. Offer a separately scheduled supervised speaking component within **two calendar days** when needed; protect the overall work budget and evidence lineage.

**Your decision:** identify the actual initial age groups, approve this delivery/appointment envelope, and state whether remote service or a materially shorter appointment is essential. Do not impose an artificial adult/16+ cutoff. If younger learners are included, their age-appropriate blueprint is part of initial scope; it is not acceptable to administer an adult instrument unchanged.

The Academic Owner determines tested section limits and accessible alternatives inside the approved service envelope. **Admissions & Student Records Manager** approves accepted identity evidence and disputed-match handling, including learners without official ID or usable unique email. Do not require ID scans by default, infer identity from shared contact data or hand a candidate a staff login. Candidate-account operations remain blocked until a legitimate identity path is qualified; no new authentication architecture is selected here.

### F03 — Validity, reassessment and appeal rights (M03/M04)

**Recommend the following local service policy, not a learning/measurement standard:**

- Result validity: **90 calendar days from assessment completion**, unless material change or reviewed error requires reassessment. Latest valid released result governs; no highest-score selection.
- Ordinary full retake: **14-calendar-day wait**, no blanket count cap. Academic Owner can authorize earlier/additional reassessment for documented learning, course mismatch, accessibility or center-caused disruption. Bank shortages cannot justify unsafe reuse.
- Targeted reassessment: for a documented uncertain or invalid component, ordinarily within **seven calendar days** of the original completion. Reuse other evidence only if still relevant, compatible and uncompromised; otherwise require full reassessment. Beyond seven days, academic review must justify freshness or request a full retake. No best-section combination.
- Restore only verified time lost during a recoverable interruption. Hold/replace affected components where timing, task fairness or custody is uncertain; preserve all events. Center-caused failures do not become low scores or ordinary retake restrictions.
- Appeals: normally within **seven calendar days after release**; allow justified late error/accessibility claims. Reassessment charges/refunds remain outside this gate and are not implicitly approved.

**Your decision:** approve or amend these learner entitlements and the Academic Owner's documented exception authority. No technical vote is required on timers, exposure algorithms or history preservation. The operational bank policy must prevent trivial repeat exposure without silently relaxing published constraints.

### F04 — Review capacity and release service (M04)

**Recommend:** qualified assessors calibrated against shared anchors; second-review every borderline/uneven recommendation, appeal, conflict, integrity issue or disagreement that could change course placement. In addition, independently rate **one in five routine speaking/writing cases**, sampled across assessors and modes. Increase review when inconsistency is found; never reduce it merely because a candidate count was reached. Numeric sampling is a proposed staffing commitment, not proof of reliability.

Every release requires a qualified approver independent of the original scorer for that case. Routine release review need not re-mark the whole test. For unrecorded speaking, sampled or flagged independent assessment needs a second live observer or a fresh documented follow-up; unavailable audio cannot be retrospectively re-rated.

**Your decision:** commit to this review capacity and a target of **two center working days for ordinary release after evidence is complete**, and **five working days for an appeal response**, or supply realistic alternatives. Holds require notice and a revised expected date; a deadline never overrides unresolved evidence. Deputies are role-based now; individual names are not required at this gate. Staffing shortage cannot waive independent release.

### F05 — Recording and records policy (M05)

**Recommend:** live speaking with structured notes as default; **optional audio-only recorded speaking** for justified assessment/moderation/appeal use, with a live route for learners who cannot appropriately be recorded. No routine video, biometric matching, marketing reuse or model training. Recording capability is not permission to collect data.

**Proposed ordinary retention schedule:** audio **90 days after release**; non-audio raw responses/scripts/scans **180 days after release**; released result, score summary, rationale, policy/form references and necessary audit history **three years after the last substantive release or case closure, whichever is later**. Abandoned/voided evidence: **90 days after closure**. Incidental access/edits do not restart these periods. The longer summary period supports disputes and course-fit explanation without retaining all sensitive media.

These are operational starting periods, **not statutory requirements**. Relevant appeals/investigations/legal holds suspend deletion under documented authority and review. Content versions needed to interpret retained decisions remain restricted; lawful evidence deletion leaves a deletion event and an explicit limit on future re-marking. Unclosed cases need a periodic closure review, not indefinite collection. Backup expiry/restoration must respect approved retention and deletion obligations; live-file deletion alone is insufficient.

**Your decision:** approve optional audio with a live alternative and the proposed schedule, or specify a different justified need; authorize **Administration / Records & Privacy Officer** to validate jurisdiction, lawful bases/notices, age/guardian authority, disclosures, retention/holds, rights requests and content licenses. Establish the applicable jurisdiction and actual learner population; do not infer them. Only center-owned/expressly licensed content may be used. Payer/contact status never grants blanket guardian access; legal limits cannot be waived by business approval. Pending this policy, no affected personal-data workflow is implementation-ready.
