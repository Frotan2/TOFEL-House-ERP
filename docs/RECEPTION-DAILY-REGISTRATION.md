# TOEFL House — Reception daily registration (staff procedure)

**Scope:** front-counter intake only. This page is for an authorized Reception login. No developer tools, JSON editing, or manual database changes are part of daily registration. Changes in PR #124 remain **TEST PACKAGE — NO REAL DATA** until release acceptance.

## Walk-in: no email or placement result
1. Sign in with your own Reception account and open **TOEFL House Reception Desk**.
2. Under **New visitors and follow-ups**, choose **Register a new visitor**.
3. Enter the person's **real full name**. Add an optional phone, interested program, preferred study mode (**Online**, **On-campus** or **Undecided**), optional country and optional learner time zone (for example `Asia/Kabul`, `Europe/London` or `America/New_York`). A worldwide online learner may sign up for Special Speaking, TOEFL Preparation or other eligible online programs without an Afghan number, email or Afghanistan residency. These are inquiry preferences, **not** validated enrollment, course scheduling or proof of country. The timezone must be a valid IANA identifier if provided; do not guess it from a country code. Every inquiry needs a real authorized Reception Branch even if the learner is abroad. With one assigned branch the backend selects it; with multiple assigned branches staff choose one. A foreign learner does not imply a foreign ERP Branch.
4. Save once. The system provides a unique internal inquiry reference and keeps an auditable operation receipt. The visitor appears in the follow-up queue.
5. Search by name, exact Inquiry ID or phone before registering another contact. Afghan mobile numbers have **exactly 10 digits beginning `07`** (`07xxxxxxxx`), or `+937xxxxxxxx` internationally, and these are equivalent for duplicate detection and search (including Dari digits). Overseas contacts from other countries use `+` and their country calling code, with 7–15 international digits in total; no foreign number is converted into an Afghan number. Country is asked separately and may differ from the phone code. The number is stored in normalized form with its national or international prefix. **Phone alone never proves identity**: family members sharing it stay separate visitors. An already-open record for the same visitor/contact/Branch should be updated instead of re-created.
6. Use **Update follow-up** to mark **Contacted**, **Awaiting Placement**, or **Closed**, adding the real next-step note. The queue displays only a short preview of the latest note. Closing a visit requires explicit confirmation; its record remains in the audit trail and is no longer editable from this queue.

**This is not student registration.** No actual Student, User, placement case, academic enrollment, receipt, invoice or tuition charge is created merely because a visitor walks in. Do not enter invented or third-party email addresses.

## International and online inquiries — front-counter safeguards
1. Online learners may be anywhere in the world. Choose **Online** for their preferred learning mode, ask their stated country (optional) and confirm their real IANA time zone if known; the phone may be a valid international `+country-code` number or blank.
2. **Do not promise Kabul-time class sessions in the learner's local time.** If the learner's time zone is missing, the Reception Desk explicitly flags it for follow-up. Even with a valid zone, a verified individual class assignment and published dated session are still required; Teaching Scheduler confirms any actual time conversion and delivery logistics.
3. On a subsequent contact, **Update follow-up** may change mode/country/time zone with optimistic version checking and audit, without silently editing name, phone, Branch or placement identity. **Correct visitor details** remains a separately reasoned action before the Placement team consumes the Inquiry.
4. An online preference never overrides course eligibility, reserved seats, academic approvals, time restrictions, Branch permissions, tuition or the student's identity requirements.

## After verified placement
1. A designated Placement Publisher must have a separately verified candidate account and create/allocate the placement case. The invigilator, assessor, reviewer and releaser execute their respective independently authorized steps.
2. A *released and still-valid* result appears in **Ready for admission intake** only when its original Placement Case has a verified branch that this Reception account may access. Expired, branchless or foreign-branch results must not appear, even if present in history. The Placement Publisher must choose the real branch when creating the case.
3. If the candidate already has a native Applicant record, **Open admission** uses that record. An already-Admitted returning applicant must have exactly one visible, enabled, natively linked Student with the same verified email; only then does **Open returning admission** prefill the Student reference. Otherwise the desk stops and asks Academic Manager to resolve the identity or branch before a new admission. For new applicants an Admission Officer uses **Record applicant** and enters the verified real first name, optionally surname, an existing native Program, academic year and authorized branch.
4. The admission then follows **Draft → Review → Approved / Conditional / Deferred / Rejected**. A reviewer and approver must have the required distinct authority.
5. **Conditional** requires an independent Admission Approver to record evidence through **Verify conditions**; do not attempt student conversion while still Conditional. The original deciding approver may not approve their own conditions.
6. Once **Approved**, an Admission Officer records offer acceptance; an Admission Approver converts into the native Student. **Enrollment Officer** then submits the real Program Enrollment.
7. Academic staff place the student in a class with available capacity. Authorized finance staff issue tuition separately under the Owner-approved billing policy, without duplicate billing.

## Student class, timetable and teacher — front-counter answers
1. In Reception Desk, search the enrolled Student by their exact Student ID, Candidate reference or verified name. Do **not** equate an initial Inquiry or an approved Applicant with a Student.
2. Submitted **Program Enrollments** establish the learner's separate **program, year and term** facts, but do **not** establish membership in any class. A learner may be in GE and Special Speaking simultaneously. Search displays distinct submitted periods and verifies each class against at least one matching enrollment, not merely whichever was registered most recently. A class with a specified academic term requires an exact matching enrollment term; an intentionally blank class term follows the native program/year-only enrollment rule. Confirm the particular period the learner is asking about before promising a class.
3. **Enrolled, class not confirmed** means no verified active roster membership in a planned or active native Student Group matches that particular submitted enrollment. A class existing for other students is not a match. Ask Teaching Scheduler to assign/confirm the learner; Reception must not change roster membership.
4. **Class planned** means this learner has an active native Student Group Student roster row in a planned class. No meeting time or permanent teacher is promised yet.
5. **Class assigned, schedule pending** means the confirmed active class has no upcoming Scheduled Course Schedule record. Ask Teaching Scheduler for the dated timetable. Do not tell the learner that the usual weekly period is already published.
6. **Class assigned, sessions scheduled** shows the class ID/name and up to three actual dated Course Schedule records: date, start/end time, course, room, and *session instructor* only when a valid native Teaching Assignment covers that class/session/date and the Instructor is active. Otherwise show **Instructor assignment unverified** and refer to Teaching Scheduler. A dated session is not a weekly recurrence or a permanent teacher assignment. Only upcoming Scheduled sessions are shown: a meeting whose end time has already passed today is no longer called the next session, and malformed times require academic verification. Cancelled/postponed meetings are not advertised.
7. If the student is **Inactive**, the enrollment read is incomplete, more than one verified class roster matches one or more submitted programs, or a timetable read is truncated, stop and refer to Academic Manager or Teaching Scheduler. Do not arbitrarily choose between a GE and Special Speaking class or guess a room, teacher or attendance status. A single class roster may still be verified even when the learner has two submitted programs. Do not reveal or act on records from another Branch.
8. An already **Scheduled** session may later overlap a class-wide suspension or an individual attendance restriction. The lookup now shows **Class sessions under review** (Teaching Scheduler) or **Attendance eligibility needs review** (Academic Manager) instead of announcing confirmed meeting/attendance. The suspension's private reason and the names of other students must not be displayed. If the suspension window is malformed or the bounded evidence is incomplete, stop and refer for verification.
9. A pending class transfer is **not** proof that the learner has moved. Reception search now shows **Transfer REQUESTED** (target class and effective date) only when both source and destination belong to its assigned Branch and the source is the learner's verified current roster. The private reason is never displayed. Multiple pending or overdue requests are referred to Academic Manager; do not promise approval. The native roster changes only after independent authorized execution on the effective date. An overdue request must be cancelled and re-requested rather than backdated. Advanced transfer types remain Owner-deferred. When the target class has prior Waiting entries, neither a policy-enabled direct move nor the independently approved transfer can take their next unoffered seat. A valid Offered seat reservation also blocks a direct move unless unreserved native capacity remains. In a rejection, the student stays in the original class; the Academic Manager must resolve the target queue first, not silently change the learner's current class. A proposed transfer effective after the target class's confirmed end date, or without a verified target end date, is rejected at request time rather than left as an impossible pending approval. An unauthorized queue bypass must not consume an approved capacity override.
10. **Available seats and class waiting list:** the native `class_availability` and `waiting_list_board` server endpoints now enforce the Reception login's explicit Branch User Permissions. A class ID, branch parameter or direct API URL cannot select a foreign branch; a Reception login without an assigned Branch gets a permission error. An academic role with a narrower Branch assignment is also scoped. Do not promise a seat or waiting-list position from a different branch, or assume an available seat equals successful enrollment. Reservation totals use a full native database count, not a clipped first page. Once the class waiting queue reaches its operational read limit, new entries require Teaching Scheduler review instead of an incomplete queue position. Expired *Offered* entries cannot be offered again by that command: withdraw them with an auditable reason, then evaluate current seats and any new admission/queue request. Queue positions are calculated from higher priority first, then the recorded request time and stable record ID. Only the first currently Waiting entry can receive the next offer; the server refuses attempts to skip that person. If they decline, record the withdrawal before proceeding to the next entry. The direct roster-add command also refuses bypassing an existing Waiting queue; admitting a waiting learner must use the auditable admit-from-waiting command. An authorized staff member cannot use direct admission from a later Waiting entry to jump the line. In the capacity response, `available` means physically unreserved seats, whereas `walk_in_available` is **zero while any Waiting entry has priority**. Use the latter when responding to a new walk-in; an existing unexpired Offered record already holds its own reserved seat. A new seat offer must never expire after the class end date: the default expiry is shortened to that date, and an explicitly later date is refused. The planning `waiting` count includes both Waiting and Offered; `waiting_to_offer` includes only unoffered Waiting entries. `projected_unmet_demand` uses the latter, so a reserved Offered learner is not counted again as unserved demand. The projection computes unmet demand **separately for each class before summing**: empty seats in a different class, timetable or Branch are not automatic substitutes for the waiting learner's requested class. `walk_in_seats_available` sums per-class front-counter availability after its own waiting priority.
11. Reception can explain and search these facts but cannot create or transfer class memberships, change teacher assignments, schedule sessions, mark attendance or alter enrollments. Those actions remain with their authorized academic roles.

## Staff safety and exceptions
- The reception intake workspace is explicitly English LTR, and a test checks authored English-only source strings. Keep authentic visitor names in their original spelling; never manufacture an English name.
- An inquiry is not linked automatically to an email-based candidate account. Only a verified identity and an authorized placement/admission journey may make that association. Never assume two matching names or shared phone numbers are the same person.
- If the result is expired, the case does not exist, the selected Program differs from approved academic placement, or required approver roles are missing: **stop and refer to Academic Manager**. Do not bypass the guards or choose arbitrary levels.
- A Reception account without at least one explicit Branch User Permission is denied access to the desk. The Owner must assign the permitted branch before that account views customer records. Placement cases without branch provenance remain hidden from Reception.
- Save your contact records only in Reception Desk. Do not write sensitive medical or family information in free-text notes; use a short operational follow-up description.
- An interrupted or repeated request should be retried from the **same dialog** so its idempotency key is preserved; opening a separate form starts a new request.
- If the desk or search responds slowly after a Save, only the newest requested snapshot should replace the screen. The active search is refreshed after a successful write; do not trust an earlier response that arrives late. Exact printed Applicant, Student and Inquiry IDs are prioritized over crowded fuzzy-name matches. Enter a complete ID or a short real name rather than a search longer than 120 characters.
- No final production readiness is implied by successful static tests. Test full Windows on a restored, synthetic-only site first, including role boundaries, same-key replay, branching, candidate verification, admission and ledger reconciliation.

## Acceptance checklist for an independent reviewer
- [ ] All UI labels, statuses, dialogs, validation messages, staff help, and developer-authored code comments in this workflow are in English. Real visitor names remain as provided.
- [ ] Search results never revert to an older query response, even when HTTP requests finish out of order.
- [ ] Double-clicking Save while one registration POST is pending cannot send two requests; a failed request can be retried with the same operation key.
- [ ] Visitor wildcard characters are treated literally, and updating a visit without any changed data does not create an audit revision.
- [ ] New, phone-free walk-in records without any email or candidate account.
- [ ] Afghan and Latin phone numerals normalize and search consistently. Full Afghan local `079...` and international `+9379...` versions return the same open contact, including when two registration requests race; shared family phones never merge different names.
- [ ] An exact printed Inquiry/Applicant/Student ID remains at the top when fuzzy matches fill the permitted first page. An overlong search is rejected before any read, not silently cut to a different identity.
- [ ] A successful visitor or follow-up POST refreshes both the main queue and an already-open search. Late older GET responses, errors and loading indicators cannot replace the newest screen.
- [ ] Same-key replay yields exactly one inquiry; different-key multi-submit is explicitly monitored.
- [ ] Other-branch Reception is unable to read, query or update the inquiry, including cached receipts.
- [ ] Invalid or expired placement cannot create admission from the queue.
- [ ] Existing applicant does not receive a duplicate-registration action.
- [ ] Conditional admission cannot convert until another approver verifies conditions with evidence.
- [ ] The real Account/Student/Program Enrollment/Fees ledger is unchanged by a preliminary inquiry.
- [ ] The release is signed, CI exact-head passes, and Owner Windows acceptance authorizes use of real data.
