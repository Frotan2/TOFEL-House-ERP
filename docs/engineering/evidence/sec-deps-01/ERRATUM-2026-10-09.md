Correction to the mitigation text. The override gate's logic is what the
register describes, but the wrapper cannot succeed as written. It forwards
`format=`, `doc=` and `no_letterhead=` to the vendor functions, and the pinned
vendor signatures at `frappe/utils/weasyprint.py` lines 11 and 22 (frappe
`988e54f3`) do not accept them, so every call that passes the gate raises
`TypeError`. This is fail-closed. It is a static reading of the signatures, not
an executed test. The vendor `printview` and `attach_print` paths bypass the
wrapper for beta formats (README section 7, risk 3). So WeasyPrint's MITIGATED
label rests on the build-time Ghostscript guard, not on the override gate alone.

   `py:oauthlib@3.3.1`: GHSA-hj66-6f7g-4r5v and PYSEC-2026-4113. The CI OSV feed
   lists both; the PyPI per-release JSON omits them. GHSA-hj66 is NOT_REACHABLE in
   `advisory-delta-2026-09-29`. PYSEC-2026-4113 is NOT_REACHABLE in
   `advisory-delta-2026-10-02`, which records it as the mirror of GHSA-hj66 with the
   same vulnerable range. The PyPI records for oauthlib 3.3.1 that the replay does
   include are GHSA-xpv3-w29h-x7cv and PYSEC-2026-4114, and both are already counted
